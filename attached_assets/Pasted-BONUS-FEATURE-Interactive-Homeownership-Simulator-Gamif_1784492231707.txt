BONUS FEATURE: Interactive Homeownership Simulator (Gamified Learning Experience)

Create an optional interactive educational experience called:

🏡 Homeownership Simulator

or

🎮 Path to Your First Home

This should be an engaging browser-based experience that teaches first-time homebuyers the homebuying process through gameplay.

The game should feel fun, educational, modern, and rewarding—not like a quiz.

It should appeal especially to Gen Z and Millennial users.

Overall Goal

Players begin as someone renting an apartment and work toward purchasing their first home.

Throughout the game they make realistic decisions that affect:

Budget
Credit
Savings
Home options
Mortgage approval
Closing process
Homeownership success

By the end, players understand the entire homebuying journey.

Game Style

Create a modern browser-based simulation.

Prefer lightweight 3D if possible using technologies such as:

Three.js
React Three Fiber
Babylon.js

If full 3D becomes too resource intensive, create an isometric or 2.5D version instead.

Gameplay should remain smooth on desktop and mobile.

Game Progression

The player starts in a small rental apartment.

Mission:

Save enough money to buy a home.

Complete realistic homeownership milestones.

Unlock a new home.

Decorate it.

Continue improving it.

Stage 1
Renting

The player begins renting.

Tasks include:

Pay rent

Track monthly expenses

Build savings

Improve credit score

Reduce debt

Choose between spending and saving

Unexpected life events may occur.

Examples:

Car repair

Medical bill

Job promotion

Bonus paycheck

Higher rent

The player learns budgeting.

Stage 2
Preparing to Buy

Player begins researching homes.

Activities include:

Searching listings

Comparing neighborhoods

Learning about affordability

Meeting a housing counselor

Improving credit

Exploring grants

Comparing mortgage options

Learning about down payments

Checking eligibility for assistance

The simulator teaches why each step matters.

Stage 3
House Hunting

Players browse homes.

Each property includes:

Price

Photos

Neighborhood

Commute

Schools

Parks

Restaurants

Crime rating (placeholder)

Walkability

Estimated mortgage

Monthly payment

Property taxes

Insurance

HOA

Maintenance estimate

Players compare homes before making an offer.

Stage 4
Offer & Negotiation

Players choose:

Offer price

Inspection

Negotiation strategy

Earnest money

Contingencies

Possible outcomes:

Offer accepted

Counteroffer

Rejected

Competing buyers

Inspection issues

Repairs requested

Stage 5
Mortgage Approval

Player submits documents.

Complete simple educational tasks explaining:

Income verification

Credit

Debt-to-income ratio

Loan approval

Interest rates

Mortgage types

Closing costs

Loan estimate

Players receive approval or suggestions for improvement.

Stage 6
Closing Day

Create an exciting closing experience.

Player signs documents.

Receives keys.

Celebration animation.

Confetti.

Achievement unlocked:

🏡 First-Time Homeowner

Stage 7
Move In

Player enters their new home.

This becomes the fun portion of the simulator.

Furniture Designer

Allow players to decorate.

Include categories:

Living Room

Bedroom

Kitchen

Dining Room

Bathroom

Office

Outdoor

Garage

Players can:

Move furniture

Rotate furniture

Change colors

Choose styles

Modern

Traditional

Farmhouse

Furniture snaps naturally into rooms.

Players can browse:

Smart Budgeting

Teach users about financial decisions.

Examples:

"You only have $4,000 remaining."

"Would you rather purchase a premium sofa or save money for future repairs?"

Show how spending choices affect savings.

Home Maintenance

Occasionally introduce events such as:

Roof leak

Broken water heater

HVAC replacement

Lawn care

Painting

Appliance replacement

Teach homeowners about maintenance costs.

Home Improvement

Players can later upgrade:

Kitchen

Bathrooms

Flooring

Landscaping

Solar panels

Garage

Patio

Energy efficiency

Each improvement increases home value.

Include short activities such as:

Credit Score Builder

Budget Challenge

Closing Cost Calculator

Inspection Challenge

Neighborhood Comparison

Grant Finder

Down Payment Savings Challenge

These should be fun, interactive, and educational.

🏆 Dream Home

Community Exploration

Allow players to explore Newport News.

Visit:

Parks

Coffee shops

Restaurants

Waterfront

Community events

Employers

Real estate office

These locations teach users about local resources.

Integration with the Website

The simulator should integrate naturally with the Housing Hub.

Examples:

Eligibility Quiz unlocks recommended assistance programs.

Neighborhood Explorer influences where players shop for homes.

Affordability Calculator helps determine purchasing power.

Housing Journey tracks overall progress.

Resources page provides educational help throughout the game.

The simulator should reinforce—not replace—the educational tools already built into the website.

Design Style

Maintain the same branding, colors, typography, and overall visual identity as the Housing Hub website.

The simulator should feel like an extension of the website—not a separate product.

Educational Goal

Every mechanic in the simulator should teach a real concept about renting, budgeting, buying, financing, closing, homeownership, or maintaining a home.

Players should leave with a much better understanding of the homebuying process while having fun.

The experience should feel similar to a blend of:

The Sims (home decorating)
Animal Crossing (cozy progression)
House Flipper (customization)
Zillow (home shopping)
Monopoly (financial decision-making)
