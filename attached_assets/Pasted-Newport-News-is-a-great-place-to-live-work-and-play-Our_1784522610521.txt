Strategic Housing Initiatives Plan
In accordance with findings in the City’s Comprehensive Housing Study and in alignment with the City’s Strategic Housing Priorities within the City’s Strategic Plan, a multi-departmental working group including staff from the City Manager’s Office, Planning, Development, and Codes as well as Newport News Redevelopment and Housing Authority have developed the Strategic Housing Initiatives Plan (SHIP).

The SHIP was developed to serve as a practical “roadmap” for translating policy ideas into actionable strategies. These strategies are arranged in areas including housing policy & regulatory reform, housing production & redevelopment, housing affordability & access, and preservation, maintenance & neighborhood stability and include active/ongoing strategies. Incorporated within are approaches such as creating programs supporting small and local developers, strengthening code enforcement tools like rental inspection programs and vacant property registries, and enhancing homeownership and housing rehabilitation opportunities.

In recognition of the City’s wide-ranging needs associated with housing, the SHIP covers affordable, workforce, market-rate, senior, family housing, and more to not only address essential livability needs but also support and spur appropriate economic development which relies on attractive and attainable housing to thrive.

The SHIP, through partnership and engagement with developers, community organizations, institutions, and residents, serves to ensure achievable housing strategies responsive to local needs. With careful implementation, the SHIP will help to continually foster a strong, healthy, and vibrant Newport News community through striving towards attainable housing opportunities for all.
