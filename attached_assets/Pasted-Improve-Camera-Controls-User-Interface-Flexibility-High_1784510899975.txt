Improve Camera Controls & User Interface Flexibility (High Priority)

The simulator should give players more control over both the camera and the interface.

Currently, the objective panels and dialogue windows remain fixed on the screen and often block important parts of the environment.

The camera also feels too restrictive.

The experience should feel much more interactive and player-controlled.

Draggable User Interface

All major UI windows should be draggable.

Examples:

Objectives
Budget panel
Dialogue windows
Tutorial panels
Inventory
Character information
Achievement notifications (where appropriate)

Players should be able to:

Click and drag any window.
Reposition it anywhere on the screen.
Move it out of the way while exploring.
Have it remember its last position during the current session.
Minimize & Expand

Large panels should include controls to:

Minimize
Collapse
Expand
Pin
Close (when appropriate)

For example, the Objectives panel could collapse into a small icon that expands again with one click.

This prevents the UI from covering the game world.

Camera Controls

Expand the camera controls to make exploration feel more natural.

Allow players to:

Rotate left and right (Y-axis)
Tilt the camera up and down (X-axis)
Zoom in
Zoom out
Pan slightly around the player

The camera should always remain centered on the player while giving enough freedom to comfortably inspect buildings, rooms, furniture, and NPCs.

Mouse Controls

Support intuitive controls such as:

Left-click to move and interact.
Click and drag to rotate the camera.
Mouse wheel to zoom.
Hold the right mouse button and drag to orbit the camera around the player.
Double-click to move quickly to a destination (optional).

Movement and camera controls should feel smooth and responsive.

Camera Limits

Prevent the camera from:

Clipping through walls.
Going below the ground.
Rotating into awkward angles.
Losing sight of the player.

Maintain sensible limits while still allowing players to look around comfortably.

Better Object Viewing

Allow players to inspect important objects more easily.

Examples:

Houses
Furniture
Neighborhoods
Parks
Documents
NPCs

The improved camera should make exploring these areas enjoyable instead of frustrating.

Automatic UI Behavior

When entering a conversation or interacting with an object:

Move pop-up panels to a location that does not block the interaction.
Reposition tooltips automatically if they overlap with another panel.
Avoid covering important NPCs or interactive objects.

The interface should intelligently adapt to what the player is doing.
