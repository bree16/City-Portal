Inclusive Community & Lifestyle Experience (Very Important)

The website should not only help users find housing—it should help them imagine building a life in Newport News.

Throughout the entire website, create an inviting, welcoming atmosphere that reflects a modern, diverse, and vibrant community.

Visual Storytelling

Use realistic, high-quality photography and illustrations that represent the people who actually make up Newport News and the people the city hopes to attract.

Include a diverse mix of residents such as:

Young professionals
First-time homebuyers
Renters
Military members and military families
Veterans
Families with children
Couples
Single adults
Older adults
People walking their dogs
Friends enjoying local businesses
People from different racial, ethnic, and cultural backgrounds
People with visible disabilities where appropriate

The goal is for every visitor to feel represented and welcomed.

Community Lifestyle

Throughout the website, showcase what life in Newport News actually feels like.

Use engaging images and sections featuring people:

Walking dogs through neighborhoods
Relaxing in parks
Hiking local trails
Visiting coffee shops
Dining at restaurants
Enjoying local food and culture
Shopping at local businesses
Attending festivals and community events
Volunteering
Exploring the waterfront
Exercising outdoors
Spending time with family and friends

Housing is more than purchasing a home—it is becoming part of a community.

The website should communicate that message visually.

Military-Friendly Community

Highlight that Newport News is an excellent place for military families and veterans.

Include information about:

Active-duty military relocation
Veteran resources
Military spouses
Nearby military installations
Government careers
Shipbuilding careers
Healthcare careers
Manufacturing and technology opportunities

Include a dedicated section called:

Military & Veteran Resources

Pet-Friendly Community

Many younger adults own pets.

Include subtle references throughout the website showing:

Dog-friendly parks
Walking trails
Outdoor spaces
Pet-friendly neighborhoods

This helps users picture themselves living in Newport News.

Welcoming Atmosphere

Every page should communicate that Newport News is:

Friendly
Safe
Inclusive
Diverse
Family-friendly
Military-friendly
Pet-friendly
Community-oriented
Opportunity-focused

Avoid making the website feel cold, institutional, or intimidating.

Create an Emotional Connection

When visitors explore the website, they should naturally think:

"I could picture myself living here."
"This community looks welcoming."
"There are people like me here."
"This city has opportunities for me."
"I want to learn more."

The website should inspire confidence, belonging, and excitement—not simply present information.

Interactive "Why Newport News?" Experience

Instead of simply listing facts, create an engaging experience.

Examples:

Featured neighborhoods
Local business spotlights
"A Day in Newport News" timeline
Community event calendar
Resident testimonials
Interactive map of attractions
Seasonal events
Weekend activity suggestions

The goal is to make visitors excited about living in Newport News—not just informed.

Overall Design Philosophy

The website should combine the professionalism of a city government website with the engaging experience of modern consumer platforms like Zillow, Airbnb, and Rocket Mortgage.

Every design decision should answer this question:

"Does this help someone feel informed, welcomed, and excited about living in Newport News?"
