Final Goal

This project should feel less like a government website and more like a modern digital service similar to Zillow, Rocket Mortgage, or Airbnb.

The focus is not adding more information.

The focus is making existing housing information easier to find, easier to understand, and easier to use, especially for first-time homebuyers, renters, and younger adults.

Keep all existing styling and branding while expanding the site into a polished, interactive prototype suitable for presenting to the Newport News Department of Housing & Neighborhood Services.
