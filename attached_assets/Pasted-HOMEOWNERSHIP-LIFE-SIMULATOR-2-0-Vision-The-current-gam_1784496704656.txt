HOMEOWNERSHIP LIFE SIMULATOR 2.0
Vision

The current game is an excellent proof of concept.

Instead of feeling like a collection of educational rooms, transform it into a fully immersive life simulator where the player experiences the complete journey from renting an apartment to becoming a successful homeowner.

The game should combine financial literacy, homeownership education, life simulation, and exploration into one seamless experience.

Think of it as a blend of:

The Sims
House Flipper
Animal Crossing
Zillow
Rocket Mortgage
Life Simulation games

while remaining educational and appropriate for a city government housing program.

The goal is not simply to teach housing information.

The goal is to make players emotionally experience the process of becoming a homeowner.

Overall Story

Players begin with very little.

They rent a small apartment.

They have limited savings.

Average credit.

Student loans or other debt.

A monthly budget.

Their goal is simple:

Build a better life.

Throughout the simulator they will:

Save money.

Improve credit.

Pay off debt.

Find a better job.

Learn about homeownership.

Purchase their first home.

Move in.

Decorate.

Maintain it.

Increase its value.

Eventually purchase their dream home.

Every decision affects the player's future.

Long-Term Goal

The simulator should feel like a polished educational experience that could realistically be showcased by the City of Newport News as an innovative way to educate future residents.

The experience should be memorable enough that users finish the game understanding:

How to budget.
How to improve credit.
How to qualify for assistance.
How to purchase a home.
How to furnish a home.
How to maintain a home.
How to build long-term wealth through homeownership.

The player should finish the experience not only with a virtual home, but with real-world knowledge and confidence to begin their own homeownership journey.
