Animated Newport News Logo (Homepage Feature)

I will be uploading the official Newport News logo. Please use that exact logo as the foundation for this animation rather than recreating or redesigning it.

The goal is to transform the existing logo into a premium animated brand element that immediately communicates innovation, community, and Newport News' waterfront identity while remaining professional and appropriate for a government website.

Placement

Use the animated logo in the primary website header/navigation where the Newport News branding already exists (top left).

It should become part of the site's identity rather than feeling like a separate animation.

Overall Style

I want the animation to feel:

Elegant
Modern
Organic
Premium
Calm
Memorable

Avoid flashy or gimmicky effects.

The animation should feel like something you would see on an award-winning civic or technology website.

Clay & Water Inspired Motion

Take inspiration from:

Soft clay animation
Liquid morphing
Water ripples
Gentle waves
Organic motion
Flowing gradients

The animation should reference Newport News' connection to the waterfront without becoming distracting.

Intro Animation

When someone first visits the homepage:

The logo should animate in over approximately 2–3 seconds.

Ideas include:

Colors flowing into place like liquid paint
Soft clay-like shapes merging into the logo
Water ripples revealing the logo
Gentle wave motion
Organic morphing transitions
Smooth easing with no harsh movements

After the animation completes, the logo should remain static.

Hover Interaction

When users hover over the logo:

Create a subtle interactive response.

Possible effects:

Gentle ripple spreading outward
Soft floating motion
Slight breathing animation
Flowing gradient movement
Small shimmer passing across the logo
Tiny water ripple beneath it

The hover effect should stop naturally when the cursor leaves.

Color Palette

Use the official Newport News brand colors from the uploaded logo.

Do not invent an entirely new color palette.

If accent colors are needed, derive them from the existing branding.

Performance

The animation should remain lightweight.

Use:

SVG animation
CSS transforms
Modern browser animation techniques

Avoid large video files or heavy rendering that slows page performance.

Accessibility

The logo animation must comply with WCAG 2.1 AA.

Requirements:

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
If reduced motion is enabled, display the logo without decorative animation.
No flashing or strobing effects.
Keep movement smooth and subtle.
The logo must remain fully readable at all times.
Overall Goal

The animated Newport News logo should become the visual signature of the Housing Hub. It should create a memorable first impression that feels polished, welcoming, and modern while reinforcing the city's waterfront identity. The animation should be refined enough that visitors immediately notice the attention to detail, but subtle enough that it never distracts from the website's primary purpose of helping residents navigate their housing journey.
