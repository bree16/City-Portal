Visual Style & User Experience (Very Important)

This website should not feel like a traditional government website.

Instead, design it as a modern digital experience that younger adults (ages 18–35) would actually enjoy using.

Take inspiration from the visual quality and user experience of companies like:

Zillow
Airbnb
Rocket Mortgage
Apple
Notion
Spotify
Chase Bank
Modern fintech apps

The website should immediately feel welcoming, exciting, and easy to explore—not corporate, outdated, or text-heavy.

Design Requirements
Avoid plain white pages with long paragraphs.
Break information into visually appealing cards, sections, and interactive elements.
Use bold headlines and short descriptions.
Create an experience that encourages users to explore instead of simply reading.
Modern UI Elements

Include:

Large hero images
Beautiful illustrations
Animated icons
Hover effects on every card
Smooth page transitions
Scroll animations
Animated statistics
Rounded cards
Glassmorphism where appropriate
Soft shadows
Modern gradients
Clean spacing
Micro-interactions on buttons
Smooth loading animations

The site should feel polished and premium.

Color Palette

Avoid dull government colors.

Instead use an energetic but professional palette.

Primary Colors

Navy Blue
Deep Teal
White

Accent Colors

Gold
Sky Blue
Emerald Green

Use gradients sparingly throughout the site.

Buttons should have subtle hover animations.

Cards should slightly elevate when hovered.

Typography

Use modern fonts such as:

Poppins
Inter

Use large headings and generous spacing.

Avoid walls of text.

Every section should be easy to scan in under 10 seconds.

Homepage Experience

When someone lands on the homepage they should immediately think:

"This doesn't feel like a government website."

Instead it should feel like a modern digital service.

Use full-width hero sections.

High-quality images.

Large buttons.

Interactive cards.

Small animations.

Scrolling should feel smooth and engaging.

Make the Website Feel Alive

Add subtle animations throughout the website.

Examples:

Cards lift slightly on hover.
Icons animate when hovered.
Buttons have ripple or glow effects.
Statistics count upward when scrolling into view.
Sections fade or slide into view while scrolling.
Background shapes move subtly for depth.
Floating illustrations or decorative graphics create energy without becoming distracting.

Animations should be smooth, professional, and enhance usability.

Appeal to Younger Adults

Remember that the primary audience includes Gen Z and Millennials.

Design decisions should prioritize:

Mobile-first experience
Fast access to information
Large, touch-friendly buttons
Visual storytelling
Interactive tools
Personalized recommendations
Clean layouts with minimal clicks

Avoid making the website feel like users are navigating government departments.

Instead, make it feel like they are using a modern app that helps them accomplish their goals.

Keep It Professional

Although the website should feel exciting and modern, it must still maintain credibility and trust because it represents a city government.

The final product should strike a balance between:

Professional
Friendly
Modern
Accessible
Innovative
Community-focused

The goal is for users to feel confident, welcomed, and excited about exploring housing opportunities in Newport News.
