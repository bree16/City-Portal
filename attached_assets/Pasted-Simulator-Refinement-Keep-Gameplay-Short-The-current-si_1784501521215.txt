Simulator Refinement (Keep Gameplay Short)

The current simulator is heading in the right direction.

Do not turn this into a large open-world game.

This simulator is intended as an educational demonstration that someone can complete in approximately 5–8 minutes, with each stage lasting around 45–90 seconds.

The experience should remain simple, engaging, and easy to understand.

Fix Existing Issues First

Before adding anything new:

Fix the Savings Jar interaction so clicking it always opens the Budget screen.
Verify every clickable object works correctly.
Remove any broken or incomplete interactions.
Ensure every stage can be completed without confusion.
Improve the Character Creator

The current character feels too blocky and Roblox-inspired.

Keep the same customization options, but redesign the character to be:

More rounded
More natural in proportion
More expressive
Better animated
Slightly more realistic

Aim for a style closer to The Sims than Roblox.

Do not make the graphics photorealistic or overly detailed, since the game should still run smoothly in a browser.

Improve the Rooms

Keep the current room-based gameplay.

Simply make each room feel more realistic by adding:

Better lighting
More furniture
Plants
Wall art
Rugs
Windows
Better flooring
Small decorative items

The rooms should feel lived in without becoming cluttered.

Keep the Pace Fast

Each stage should be completable in about one minute.

Players should always know exactly what to do next.

Avoid long dialogue or complicated mechanics.

Each stage should teach one main concept before moving on.

Add Small Interactive Moments

Instead of expanding into a giant game, make each stage slightly more interactive.

Examples:

Renting

Click the budget notebook.
Decide whether to save money or pay debt.
Check your savings jar.

Preparing

Talk to the counselor.
Review your credit score.
Complete the document checklist.

House Hunting

Walk through two or three homes.
Compare monthly payments.
Select your favorite.

Closing

Sign documents.
Receive the keys.
Unlock "First-Time Homeowner."

Move-In

Place a few pieces of furniture.
Choose a couch.
Pick a bed.
Hang artwork.
Finish decorating.

These interactions should take less than a minute each while still being satisfying.

Improve the UI

Keep the current interface, but make it feel more polished.

Slightly smaller dialog windows
Better spacing
Softer shadows
Smoother animations
Cleaner icons
More modern typography
Keep the Educational Focus

The simulator should feel fun, but its primary purpose is education.

Every stage should teach one important concept:

Budgeting
Saving
Credit
Home searching
Mortgage basics
Closing
Moving in
Homeownership

The player should finish the simulator with a clear understanding of the homebuying process without feeling overwhelmed.
