NEW FEATURE: Resident Services Hub

Expand the Housing Hub beyond homeownership and renting by creating a new Resident Services Hub.

The goal is to make this website the digital front door to Newport News.

Once someone finds a home, they should also be able to access everyday city services without leaving the website.

This should become one of the main sections of the Housing Hub.

Inspiration

Use the attached reference image only as inspiration for the layout and user experience.

I like the clean presentation, large circular icons, spacing, and simple navigation.

Do not copy the design exactly.

Instead, redesign it using the existing Housing Hub branding, color palette, typography, animations, and modern UI.

The new section should feel like it belongs naturally within the website.

Section Title

Create a homepage section called:

Everything You Need After Moving In

Subtitle:

Everything you need to live, work, and thrive in Newport News—all in one place.

Layout

Create a responsive grid of large interactive service cards.

Desktop:

6 cards per row

Tablet:

3 cards per row

Mobile:

2 cards per row

Cards should have:

Large circular icon
Soft shadows
Modern gradients
Rounded edges
Hover elevation
Click animation
Smooth transitions

Each card should feel modern and interactive.

👮 Public Safety

Parking, vehicle services, transportation updates, and driving resources.

🏥 Health & Wellness

Visual Design

This section should feel vibrant and welcoming.

Use:

High-quality custom icons
Beautiful illustrations
Subtle gradients
Glassmorphism accents
Soft shadows
Rounded cards
Smooth hover animations
Consistent spacing

Avoid making the section look like a traditional government directory.

Integration with the Housing Hub

This should not feel like a separate website.

It should naturally continue the user's journey.

The flow should feel like:

🏡 Find a Home

↓

📍 Explore Neighborhoods

↓

🎮 Learn Through the Simulator

↓

🚚 Move Into Your New Home

↓

🏙 Access Resident Services

↓

❤️ Become Part of the Newport News Community

The Resident Services Hub should reinforce that homeownership is only the beginning of living in Newport News.
